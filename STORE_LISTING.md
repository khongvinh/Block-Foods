## App name (≤ 30 ký tự)
Block Foods: Cook & Puzzle

## Short description (≤ 80 ký tự)
Stack ingredient blocks, cook world dishes and run your own little restaurant!

## Full description (≤ 4000 ký tự)
Welcome to Block Foods, a cozy block puzzle where every block is a tasty ingredient!

🧩 PUZZLE + COOKING
Drag ingredient blocks onto the cutting board. Fill a row or column to chop it and collect the ingredients for your recipe. Plan ahead: when the board fills up, a plate breaks!

🍜 OPEN YOUR SHOP
Customers walk in and order dishes from around the world: omelette, egg fried rice, ramen, pad thai, tacos, tom yum, spaghetti, teriyaki fish, mapo tofu, bibimbap and more. Collect the ingredients, then cook with quick mini-games: tap on time, keep the heat just right, or add ingredients in the right order.

🪙 SERVE AND COUNT THE CHANGE
Hand over the dish, count the coins back before the customer loses patience, and earn tips for perfect service. Every customer leaves a 1–5 star review. Can you keep every dish at five stars?

♾️ ENDLESS MARKET
Prefer a classic puzzle? Play endless mode, chain combos, clear lines of the same ingredient for bonus points and beat your best score.

🏆 LEADERBOARDS
Compete on the global leaderboards for best score, best ratings and total earnings. No sign-up needed: you get a fun chef name like "Chef Tomato #1234".

✨ FEATURES
• 12 world dishes to unlock, each with its own recipe card
• 8 customers with their own personalities
• 3 cooking mini-games
• Cheerful music, sound effects and vibration (can be turned off)
• Plays offline
• No ads, no in-app purchases
• Available in English, Español, Português, Bahasa Indonesia, हिन्दी and Tiếng Việt
