Block Foods – Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Block Foods ("the game") is a puzzle game made by [DEVELOPER NAME]. This page explains what data the game uses.

What we collect

  • Anonymous player ID. When the game connects to the internet it signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase Authentication. This creates a random ID. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account.
  • Game results. Best score, dishes unlocked, customer ratings, coins earned and the time of your last update. These are stored in Google Cloud Firestore so they can be shown on the public leaderboard.
  • Chef name. The game picks a name such as "Chef Tomato #1234". You cannot type your own name, so the leaderboard never contains personal information.

Settings and progress (language, sound, unlocked dishes) are also saved on your device.

What we do not collect

No name, email, contacts, photos, location, microphone, advertising ID or payment information. The game has no ads and no in-app purchases.

How the data is used

Only to show the leaderboard and to keep your progress. We do not sell or share data with anyone else. Data is processed by Google Firebase (Firebase privacy information).

Deleting your data

Tap your chef name on the home screen, then Remove me from the leaderboard. This deletes your results from the server right away. Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on the device. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
